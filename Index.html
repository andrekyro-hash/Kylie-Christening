A Healthy Celebration

Health & Safety Guide

Let's help keep our little princess and everyone celebrating with us safe and comfortable.

🤒
Please Stay Home if Unwell

If you have a fever, cough, flu symptoms, or feel unwell, please rest at home and send your love to Kylie Rae.

🧼
Practice Good Hygiene

Please wash or sanitize your hands before interacting with the baby.

😷
Wear a Mask if Needed

Guests with mild respiratory symptoms or those who prefer extra protection are encouraged to wear a well-fitting mask.

👶🏻
Handle Our Baby Gently

Please ask the parents before carrying Kylie Rae, avoid kissing her face and hands, and keep hands clean.

🤍
Respect Everyone's Comfort

Please respect each guest's personal space and any family health precautions.

💧
Stay Comfortable

Stay hydrated, follow the venue's guidelines, and help us keep the celebration clean.

💗 Thank you for helping us protect our little princess. Your understanding, kindness, and cooperation mean so much to our family.
